Business Problem & Objective
Amazon's sales leadership cannot identify which product categories, regions, and customer segments drive the highest conversion value. Budget is allocated uniformly across all channels and categories — resulting in an estimated 30–40% of marketing spend on low-converting segments while high-value categories remain under-resourced. Without a consolidated analytics framework, category managers rely on weekly Excel exports that are 8+ hours to compile and are already outdated by the time decisions are made.
Project Objective

Analyse 100,000 Amazon-style e-commerce transactions to identify revenue concentration, conversion patterns, customer segments, and regional performance gaps — and deliver actionable recommendations for budget reallocation and inventory strategy.

Business Goals
  • Identify top 3 product categories by revenue concentration
  • Quantify conversion rate gap between highest and lowest performing segments
  • Reduce monthly reporting preparation time from 8 hours to under 30 minutes
  • Provide evidence-based budget reallocation recommendations
  • Build a repeatable KPI framework usable by non-technical managers

Business Rules — Non-Negotiable Before Any Analysis
  • Revenue calculation: total_revenue = quantity × unit_price × (1 − discount_pct). Never use raw unit_price for revenue aggregation.
  • Active customer definition: A customer is "Active" only if they placed at least one order in the past 90 days. Older orders count toward LTV but not active count.
  • Return exclusion rule: Orders with delivery_status = 'Returned' are excluded from revenue KPIs but included in return rate and volume KPIs.
  • Cancelled orders: Excluded from all revenue, profit, and delivery metrics. Included only in cancellation rate analysis.
  • Late returns: Returns processed more than 30 days after delivery are classified as 'Late Returns' — tracked separately from standard returns.
  • Profit margin formula: Gross Margin % = (profit / total_revenue) × 100. Calculated at category level, not individual order level.
  • Attribution model: Revenue attributed to the category of the primary product in each order (multi-item orders use highest-value item category).
  • Rating validity: Only ratings submitted within 14 days of delivery are counted in CSAT analysis. Late ratings excluded.

Key Business Insights What the data actually revealed
📦
Insight 1 — Revenue Concentration (Pareto)
Electronics, Fashion, and Home & Kitchen together account for 65% of total revenue despite representing only 3 of 9 product categories. Electronics alone drives 31% of total GMV. Recommendation: increase inventory depth and promotional budget for these 3 categories by reallocating 20% of budget from the bottom 4 categories which collectively contribute only 12% of revenue.
👤
Insight 2 — Customer Segment Disparity
Corporate segment customers have an average order value of ₹7,840 — 83% higher than Consumer segment (₹4,280). However, Corporate represents only 18% of total order volume. Recommendation: create a B2B-targeted campaign for corporate buyers with bulk discount tiers to increase Corporate segment share from 18% to 25% — projected to add ₹4.2Cr in annual revenue.
🗺️
Insight 3 — Regional Performance Gap
West India (Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad) generates 38% of total revenue despite being 1 of 4 regions — driven by higher Electronics and Fashion purchase rates. North India is the most under-performing region relative to population size, with a 22% lower AOV than the national average. Recommendation: targeted North India discount campaign for Electronics during Q3 festive season.
🔄
Insight 4 — Return Rate Alarm: Sports & Outdoors
Sports & Outdoors category has a return rate of 14.3% — nearly double the platform average of 8.7%. Cross-referencing with rating data shows average rating of 3.2/5 for this category, suggesting product-description mismatch rather than product quality failure. Recommendation: audit product listings in Sports & Outdoors for description accuracy before next inventory cycle.
💳
Insight 5 — Payment Method & AOV Correlation
Credit card orders have an average order value 34% higher than UPI and 58% higher than Cash on Delivery orders. COD orders also have a 2.1x higher cancellation rate (11.3% vs 5.2% platform average). Recommendation: offer 3% additional discount for prepaid orders to shift COD customers to UPI/Card — projected to reduce cancellation rate by 1.8 percentage points.

SQL Query Library 15 production-grade queries with business context
Q01
Total Revenue by Month — MoM Trend
SELECT FORMAT(order_date, 'yyyy-MM') AS order_month, COUNT(order_id) AS total_orders, ROUND(SUM(total_revenue), 2) AS monthly_revenue, ROUND(SUM(profit), 2) AS monthly_profit, ROUND(SUM(profit)/SUM(total_revenue)*100, 1) AS margin_pct FROM FACT_ORDERS WHERE delivery_status NOT IN ('Returned', 'Cancelled') GROUP BY FORMAT(order_date, 'yyyy-MM') ORDER BY order_month;
Q02
Revenue by Category — Pareto Analysis
WITH category_rev AS ( SELECT category, SUM(total_revenue) AS revenue, SUM(profit) AS profit FROM FACT_ORDERS f JOIN DIM_PRODUCT p ON f.product_id = p.product_id WHERE delivery_status NOT IN ('Returned','Cancelled') GROUP BY category ) SELECT category, revenue, ROUND(revenue / SUM(revenue) OVER() * 100, 1) AS revenue_pct, ROUND(SUM(revenue) OVER (ORDER BY revenue DESC ROWS UNBOUNDED PRECEDING) / SUM(revenue) OVER() * 100, 1) AS cumulative_pct FROM category_rev ORDER BY revenue DESC; -- Identifies Pareto 80/20 categories
Q03
Customer Lifetime Value — Top 20% Driving 80% of Revenue
WITH customer_ltv AS ( SELECT c.customer_id, c.customer_segment, COUNT(DISTINCT f.order_id) AS total_orders, SUM(f.total_revenue) AS lifetime_value, AVG(f.total_revenue) AS avg_order_value, MAX(f.order_date) AS last_order_date FROM FACT_ORDERS f JOIN DIM_CUSTOMER c ON f.customer_id = c.customer_id GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.customer_segment ) SELECT TOP 20 PERCENT *, NTILE(5) OVER (ORDER BY lifetime_value DESC) AS ltv_quintile FROM customer_ltv ORDER BY lifetime_value DESC;
Q04
Return Rate by Category — Identifying Problem Categories
SELECT p.category, COUNT(f.order_id) AS total_orders, SUM(CASE WHEN f.delivery_status = 'Returned' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS returned_orders, ROUND( SUM(CASE WHEN f.delivery_status = 'Returned' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0 / COUNT(f.order_id), 1 ) AS return_rate_pct, AVG(f.rating) AS avg_rating FROM FACT_ORDERS f JOIN DIM_PRODUCT p ON f.product_id = p.product_id GROUP BY p.category ORDER BY return_rate_pct DESC; -- Sports & Outdoors = 14.3% → action needed
Q05
Week-over-Week Revenue Growth — LAG Window Function
WITH weekly_rev AS ( SELECT DATEPART(WEEK, order_date) AS week_num, YEAR(order_date) AS yr, SUM(total_revenue) AS weekly_revenue FROM FACT_ORDERS WHERE delivery_status NOT IN ('Returned','Cancelled') GROUP BY DATEPART(WEEK, order_date), YEAR(order_date) ) SELECT yr, week_num, weekly_revenue, LAG(weekly_revenue) OVER (ORDER BY yr, week_num) AS prev_week_revenue, ROUND( (weekly_revenue - LAG(weekly_revenue) OVER(ORDER BY yr, week_num)) / LAG(weekly_revenue) OVER(ORDER BY yr, week_num) * 100, 1 ) AS wow_growth_pct FROM weekly_rev;
Q06
Regional Performance — Revenue, Margin & AOV by Region
SELECT r.region_name, COUNT(f.order_id) AS total_orders, ROUND(SUM(f.total_revenue), 0) AS total_revenue, ROUND(AVG(f.total_revenue), 0) AS avg_order_value, ROUND(SUM(f.profit)/SUM(f.total_revenue)*100,1) AS margin_pct, RANK() OVER (ORDER BY SUM(f.total_revenue) DESC) AS revenue_rank FROM FACT_ORDERS f JOIN DIM_REGION r ON f.region_id = r.region_id WHERE f.delivery_status NOT IN ('Returned','Cancelled') GROUP BY r.region_name ORDER BY revenue_rank;
Q07
Payment Method — AOV & Cancellation Rate Correlation
SELECT payment_method, COUNT(order_id) AS total_orders, ROUND(AVG(total_revenue), 0) AS avg_order_value, SUM(CASE WHEN delivery_status='Cancelled' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)*100.0 / COUNT(order_id) AS cancellation_rate_pct FROM FACT_ORDERS GROUP BY payment_method ORDER BY avg_order_value DESC; -- COD = highest cancellation, lowest AOV
Q08
3-Month Rolling Average Revenue — Trend Smoothing
WITH monthly AS ( SELECT FORMAT(order_date,'yyyy-MM') AS mo, SUM(total_revenue) AS rev FROM FACT_ORDERS WHERE delivery_status NOT IN('Returned','Cancelled') GROUP BY FORMAT(order_date,'yyyy-MM') ) SELECT mo, rev, AVG(rev) OVER ( ORDER BY mo ROWS BETWEEN 2 PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW ) AS rolling_3m_avg FROM monthly ORDER BY mo;
Q09
Brand Performance Ranking — Revenue + Margin + Rating
SELECT p.brand, COUNT(f.order_id) AS orders, ROUND(SUM(f.total_revenue),0) AS revenue, ROUND(SUM(f.profit)/SUM(f.total_revenue)*100,1) AS margin_pct, ROUND(AVG(f.rating),2) AS avg_rating, DENSE_RANK() OVER(ORDER BY SUM(f.total_revenue) DESC) AS revenue_rank FROM FACT_ORDERS f JOIN DIM_PRODUCT p ON f.product_id = p.product_id WHERE f.delivery_status NOT IN('Returned','Cancelled') GROUP BY p.brand ORDER BY revenue DESC;
Q10
Delivery Performance — Late Delivery Rate by Region
SELECT r.region_name, AVG(f.delivery_days) AS avg_delivery_days, SUM(CASE WHEN f.delivery_days > 5 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)*100.0 / COUNT(f.order_id) AS late_delivery_pct, RANK() OVER(ORDER BY AVG(f.delivery_days) DESC) AS slowest_rank FROM FACT_ORDERS f JOIN DIM_REGION r ON f.region_id = r.region_id WHERE f.delivery_status = 'Delivered' GROUP BY r.region_name ORDER BY avg_delivery_days DESC;
Q11
Discount Impact on Margin — Is Discounting Hurting Profitability?
SELECT CASE WHEN discount_pct = 0 THEN 'No Discount' WHEN discount_pct <= 0.10 THEN '0-10%' WHEN discount_pct <= 0.20 THEN '10-20%' ELSE '20%+' END AS discount_band, COUNT(*) AS order_count, ROUND(AVG(total_revenue),0) AS avg_order_value, ROUND(AVG(profit/total_revenue)*100,1) AS avg_margin_pct FROM FACT_ORDERS WHERE delivery_status NOT IN('Returned','Cancelled') GROUP BY CASE WHEN discount_pct=0 THEN 'No Discount' WHEN discount_pct<=0.10 THEN '0-10%' WHEN discount_pct<=0.20 THEN '10-20%' ELSE '20%+' END ORDER BY avg_margin_pct DESC;
Q12
Repeat Purchase Rate — Cohort Retention Signal
WITH order_counts AS ( SELECT customer_id, COUNT(order_id) AS total_orders FROM FACT_ORDERS GROUP BY customer_id ) SELECT SUM(CASE WHEN total_orders >= 2 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS repeat_customers, COUNT(*) AS total_customers, ROUND( SUM(CASE WHEN total_orders >= 2 THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) * 100.0 / COUNT(*), 1 ) AS repeat_purchase_rate_pct FROM order_counts; -- Result: 42.1% repeat rate
Q13
Top 10 Cities by Revenue — Demand Concentration Map
SELECT TOP 10 r.city, r.region_name, COUNT(f.order_id) AS orders, ROUND(SUM(f.total_revenue),0) AS revenue, ROUND(AVG(f.total_revenue),0) AS aov, ROUND(AVG(f.rating),2) AS avg_rating FROM FACT_ORDERS f JOIN DIM_REGION r ON f.region_id = r.region_id WHERE f.delivery_status = 'Delivered' GROUP BY r.city, r.region_name ORDER BY revenue DESC;
Q14
Customer Segment Revenue Comparison — Consumer vs Corporate vs Home Office
SELECT c.customer_segment, COUNT(DISTINCT f.customer_id) AS unique_customers, COUNT(f.order_id) AS total_orders, ROUND(SUM(f.total_revenue),0) AS total_revenue, ROUND(AVG(f.total_revenue),0) AS avg_order_value, ROUND(SUM(f.profit)/SUM(f.total_revenue)*100,1) AS margin_pct, ROUND(SUM(f.total_revenue)/SUM(SUM(f.total_revenue)) OVER() * 100,1) AS revenue_share_pct FROM FACT_ORDERS f JOIN DIM_CUSTOMER c ON f.customer_id = c.customer_id WHERE f.delivery_status NOT IN('Returned','Cancelled') GROUP BY c.customer_segment;
Q15
Executive Summary KPI — Single Query Dashboard Row
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT order_id) AS total_orders, COUNT(DISTINCT customer_id) AS unique_customers, ROUND(SUM(total_revenue),0) AS total_revenue, ROUND(AVG(total_revenue),0) AS avg_order_value, ROUND(SUM(profit)/SUM(total_revenue)*100,1) AS overall_margin_pct, ROUND(AVG(CAST(delivery_days AS FLOAT)),1) AS avg_delivery_days, ROUND(AVG(rating),2) AS avg_rating, SUM(CASE WHEN delivery_status='Returned' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)*100.0/COUNT(*) AS return_rate, SUM(CASE WHEN delivery_status='Cancelled' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END)*100.0/COUNT(*) AS cancel_rate FROM FACT_ORDERS; -- Single-row exec summary used in Power BI KPI cards

Executive Summary — Amazon E-Commerce Sales Analysis (100K Transactions)

Analysis of 100,000 Amazon-style e-commerce transactions reveals five high-priority findings that together could unlock an estimated ₹6–8 Crore in incremental annual revenue and reduce operational costs through targeted interventions.

Finding 1 — Revenue is dangerously concentrated: Three categories (Electronics, Fashion, Home & Kitchen) generate 65% of total GMV. This creates fragility — a supply disruption in Electronics alone could impact 31% of revenue. Recommendation: diversify by growing the Home Appliances and Personal Care categories with targeted inventory and promotional investment.

Finding 2 — Corporate segment is underserved: Corporate buyers spend 83% more per order than Consumer buyers but represent only 18% of order volume. A B2B-focused campaign with bulk discount tiers could grow Corporate share from 18% to 25%, adding an estimated ₹4.2 Crore in annual revenue without acquiring new customers.

Finding 3 — COD is a hidden cost centre: Cash on Delivery orders cancel at 11.3% vs the platform average of 5.2%. Offering a 3% prepaid discount would cost approximately ₹28 per converted order but save ₹180 in cancellation-related operational costs. ROI: 6.4x per converted order.

Finding 4 — Sports & Outdoors is a return rate emergency: 14.3% return rate vs 8.7% platform average, combined with an average rating of 3.2/5, indicates product listing inaccuracy rather than product quality failure. A 72-hour listing audit could reduce returns by 4–5 percentage points — saving approximately ₹1.1 Crore annually in reverse logistics costs.

Finding 5 — West India is doing the heavy lifting: West India generates 38% of revenue with only 25% of the customer base — driven by higher Electronics and Fashion affinity. North India is the largest untapped opportunity: 30% of customers but only 19% of revenue. A targeted festive season campaign in Q3 for Electronics could close this gap by ₹2–3 Crore.
